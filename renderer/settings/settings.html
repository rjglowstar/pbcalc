Settings

Appearance

Mode
Choose how PBCalc looks. “Device” follows your Windows app mode.
Show bookmarks bar
Shows your bookmarks under the address bar (Ctrl+Shift+B).

Address bar

Show search suggestions from Google
While you type in the address bar, what you typed is sent to Google to fetch suggestions, as in Chrome. No cookies are sent and nothing is saved. Your bookmarks and open tabs are suggested either way. Turn off to keep every keystroke on this computer.

Downloads

Location
Ask where to save each file before downloading
Off, as in Chrome: files go straight to the folder above, and an existing name gets "(1)" added instead of being overwritten. Turn on to pick a location every time.

Restricted Mode

Only open your list of sites
In Restricted Mode people can open only the sites in your bookmarks. There is no address bar and bookmarks cannot be changed. Add the sites you want to allow as bookmarks first.
Default Start Restricted
When on, PBCalc always starts in Restricted Mode, even if an administrator switched it off during the last session. When off, it starts normally.

Privacy

Browsing history
PBCalc never records the pages you visit. Cookies, cache and site data are also erased every time you close the browser. Only your bookmarks, saved passwords and these settings are kept.
